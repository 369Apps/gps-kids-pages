The GPS Kids Way

Protect the vibe.

Any coach or helper can run the plan. These six rules keep the room safe, playful, and brave.

01

Silly is brave.

Play is not a distraction—it’s how kids loosen up enough to try.

02

Every kid pitches. Every week.

A whisper-pitch counts. Standing beside the coach counts. Never skip a kid.

03

Compliments get specific.

Replace “good job” with what you noticed: the strong opening, clear voice, funny name, or useful idea.

04

A freeze means the brain is working.

No rescuing or rushing. Give them any word to start, then let the next word come.

05

Energy comes from the coach first.

Model the answer. Be willing to look silly. Clap like it matters—because the room follows you.

06

The pledge is never skipped.

Whatever else changes, protect the final five minutes: brave-moment roll call, confidence-card mark, then the pledge.

Confidence parents can see

Make every brave rep visible

Every class

During brave-moment roll call, stamp or mark one moment on each kid’s take-home confidence card. Write a few real words when useful: “strong opening,” “tried again,” or “clear voice.”

Once a month

Invite parents into the last 10 minutes. They watch the pitches live, hear the next-theme tease, and join the applause before the class closes together with the pledge.

Same 60-minute rhythm. New theme, one more brave rep, and a reason to come back.
