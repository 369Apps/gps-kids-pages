How it works

  1. Get the game plan. Every club night has a ready-made plan: the skill, the phases, the exact words to say. Open it on your phone and follow it.
  2. Run the 5 phases. Welcome, warm-up game, build block, showtime where every kid performs, then the close. 60 minutes, timer included.
  3. Close with the pledge. Every club ends the same way: loud, on their feet, the GPS Kids Pledge. The kid with the bravest moment leads it.
  4. Families play daily. Between club nights, each family plays the 1 minute speaking challenge in GPS Kids Speak. The club is the event; the app is the habit.

Questions parents ask

Do I need teaching or coaching experience?

No. The game plan tells you what to do and what to say, minute by minute. Your job is energy, not expertise.

What ages?

6 to 12. The plan includes adjustments for younger and older kids.

What does it cost?

The club game plan and the app are free. You bring the living room and the snacks.

How often should the club meet?

Once or twice a week, 60 minutes. The daily 1 minute challenge keeps the habit alive between meetings.
