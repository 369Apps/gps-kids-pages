Evergreen 12-week cycle

Every week, a brave new mission.

Kids build, play, solve and take the stage. Parents see the confidence grow—not in a report card, but in what their child can do.

The rule: every child speaks every class. A whisper still counts as a rep.

The 60-minute rhythm

Keep the structure familiar so kids feel safe. Change the mission so they stay excited.

Week 1 · Invent & Pitch

Fix-It Inventions

Spot an everyday annoyance, build a quick fix, and pitch it in 60 seconds.

    Co-coach lane: protect the vibe

    Lead coach: owns the room, the story and the energy.

    • Set materials before kids arrive.
    • Time the rounds and cue the next speaker.
    • Encourage quietly: “I want to hear your idea.”
    • Give one specific compliment after each turn.
    • Avoid: correcting mid-speech, long explanations, finishing a child’s sentence, or turning the mission into schoolwork.

    The confidence ladder

    Progress is based on observable behaviors. A child can move up, down, and back up. That’s normal.

    1
    Joins inSays one word or speaks with a coach.
    2
    Speaks clearlyFinishes a short thought the room can hear.
    3
    Adds structureUses a beginning, middle and end—or a reason.
    4
    ConnectsLooks up, listens and responds to the audience.
    5
    LeadsHosts, helps a peer or takes the stage by choice.

    The three promises

    Fun before polish

    Silly lowers the stakes. Kids speak more when they’re playing, not performing for approval.

    One visible rep

    Every child leaves with one brave moment a parent can understand and celebrate.

    Feedback that builds

    Name what worked. Give only one next step. Never turn the ride home into a critique session.

    Silly is brave. Small reps become strong voices.
